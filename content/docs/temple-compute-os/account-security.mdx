## Two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication (MFA) asks for a code from an authenticator app when
you sign in.

1. Open **Settings** and select the **Security** tab.
2. Turn on two-factor authentication and enter your password.
3. Add the secret to your authenticator app, then enter the 6-digit code it
   shows.
4. Save the 10 recovery codes. Each works once, if you lose access to the app.

On the same tab you can create new recovery codes or turn MFA off. Both ask for
a code or a recovery code.

Platform administrators must use MFA. An organization admin can also require it
for every member: on the organization's page, turn on **Require two-factor
authentication**. Members without MFA set it up at their next sign-in.

## Sessions

- Sign-in lasts while you use the app. It ends after 8 hours without activity
  and after 7 days in total.
- **Sign out everywhere** in **Settings**, **Security** ends every session of
  your account.
- Changing or resetting your password ends your other sessions. It also deletes
  your personal MCP tokens and connector grants, so reconnect your MCP clients
  afterwards.

## Change your email address

1. Open **Settings** and enter the new address in your profile.
2. Confirm with your password.
3. Open the link sent to the new address, while signed in. The link works for
   48 hours.

The address changes when you open the link.
